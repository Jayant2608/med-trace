Every batch, from factory to customer.

MedTrace follows each medicine batch through shipments, stock and sales. Recalled, fake or expired batches are blocked from sale, and anyone can check a batch code.

  • Live MySQL database medicine_traceability
  • 12 tables, 2 views, 3 triggers
  • Batch verification, trace and recall workflows
